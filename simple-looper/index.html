Click an empty pad to load a WAV or MP3. Drop audio files on a pad to load from there, or on the unit to fill the bank.
Left-click a pad to play it; right-click a pad to select it without playing.
Select a pad and click GATE or LOOP to toggle playback mode (defaults to full length), or DEL to clear the pad.
On the waveform, left-click to set the start, right-click to set the end, or drag to select a range.
REC while PLAYING records pad hits into the pattern, quantized to the 16th-note grid.
SONG loads the configured audio URLs and plays its sections once in order; main PLAY starts from the selected section, or the first section if none is selected. Add predefined song manifests to the SONGS catalog in the source.
